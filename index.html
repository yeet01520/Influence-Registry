How It Works
One score. Traceable to the filing.
Every member gets an influence score from 0 to 100. A higher score means more of their funding comes from organized money rather than individual donors.
01

Pull The Filings

We start with each member's own FEC campaign finance reports, which show how much they raised in total and how much came from individuals versus PACs and committees.

02

Separate The Money

Sorted the money into two buckets: grassroots and individual donations on one side, corporate PACs and special-interest funding on the other, broken down sector by sector.

03

Score The Reliance

The score reflects how heavily a member leans on organized money, from corporate PACs and special interests, rather than donations from individual people. The same formula runs on every member with no hand-tuning.
